## Business glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Net revenue | Product sales minus cancellations. Excludes postage, fees and manual adjustments. |
| Gross sales | Product sales before cancellations. |
| Cancellation / return | An invoice prefixed 'C' reversing earlier lines; the source has no separate returns system. |
| Order | A non-cancelled invoice with at least one product line. |
| Active customer | Identified customer with at least one order in the period. |
| New customer | Customer whose first-ever order is in the period (data starts Dec 2009 - left-censored). |
| Retention (MoM) | Share of last month's active customers active again this month. |
| Repeat purchase rate | Share of active customers whose order was not their first. |
| Churn (model) | No purchase in the 90 days after the scoring date. |
| CLV (180d) | Predicted net revenue in the next 180 days. |
| Guest | Line or order without a Customer ID (customer_key = -1). |
| Quarantine | Rows failing an error-level data-quality rule; excluded from silver/gold, kept for audit. |
| Synthetic data | Generated demonstration data (inventory, marketing); never real company performance. |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue / marketing cost (synthetic only). |
| Partial month | Month not fully covered by data (Dec 2011 ends on the 9th). |

## Metric definitions

| Key | Name | Definition | Calculation | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `net_revenue` | Net revenue | Product sales net of cancellations/returns, GBP. | SUM(line_revenue) over product lines incl. cancellation lines (negative). | actual |
| `gross_sales` | Gross sales | Product sales before cancellations, GBP. | SUM(line_revenue) over non-cancelled product lines. | actual |
| `returns_value` | Returns / cancellations value | Value of cancelled product lines, GBP. | -SUM(line_revenue) over cancellation product lines. | actual |
| `return_rate` | Return rate | Share of gross sales value later cancelled. | returns_value / gross_sales. | actual |
| `orders` | Total orders | Distinct non-cancelled invoices containing products. | COUNT(DISTINCT invoice_no) where not cancellation and product line. | actual |
| `avg_order_value` | Average order value | Gross sales per order, GBP. | gross_sales / orders. | actual |
| `units_sold` | Units sold | Units on non-cancelled product lines. | SUM(quantity). | actual |
| `active_customers` | Active customers | Identified customers with >=1 non-cancelled order in period. | COUNT(DISTINCT customer_key) excluding guests (-1). | actual |
| `new_customers` | Customer acquisition (new customers) | Customers whose first-ever order falls in the period. | COUNT(DISTINCT customer_key) where order_seq = 1. | actual |
| `repeat_purchase_rate` | Repeat purchase rate | Share of active customers in the period who had purchased before the period's order. | returning customers / active customers. | actual |
| `retention_rate` | Customer retention (month-over-month) | Share of customers active in month m-1 who are active again in month m (average over period months). | retained_customers / prev_active_customers, averaged. | actual |
| `gross_profit` | Gross profit | Revenue minus cost of goods sold. | Requires unit cost - not present in the source data. | unavailable |
| `inventory_turnover` | Inventory turnover (units, annualised) | Actual units sold in last 365 days / units on hand in the SYNTHETIC snapshot. | SUM(units_last_365d_actual) / SUM(stock_qty). | synthetic |
| `stockout_rate` | Stockout rate | Share of product-warehouse positions with zero stock (SYNTHETIC snapshot). | COUNT(stock_qty = 0) / COUNT(*). | synthetic |
| `marketing_conversion_rate` | Marketing conversion rate | Conversions / clicks (SYNTHETIC campaigns). | SUM(conversions) / SUM(clicks). | synthetic |
| `roas` | Return on advertising spend | Attributed revenue / campaign cost (SYNTHETIC campaigns). | SUM(attributed_revenue) / SUM(cost). | synthetic |
| `cac` | Customer acquisition cost (campaign) | Campaign cost / conversions (SYNTHETIC campaigns). | SUM(cost) / SUM(conversions). | synthetic |
